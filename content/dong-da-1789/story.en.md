# Ngọc Hồi – Đống Đa (1789)

_In early spring of the year Kỷ Dậu (1789), the Tây Sơn army under Quang Trung marched north, defeated the Qing army at Hà Hồi, Ngọc Hồi and Đống Đa, and entered Thăng Long._

In early spring of the year Kỷ Dậu (1789), the Tây Sơn army under Quang Trung marched north, defeated the Qing army at Hà Hồi, Ngọc Hồi and Đống Đa, and entered Thăng Long.

## The Qing army enters Thăng Long
<!-- id: cau-vien | kicker: The year Mậu Thân, 1788 -->

In 1788, King Lê Chiêu Thống left Thăng Long and went to Qing China (nước Thanh) to ask for help in keeping his throne. The Qing court sent the governor-general Sun Shiyi (Tôn Sĩ Nghị) with an army. On 21 November 1788, according to modern sources, the Qing army entered Thăng Long.

According to Việt Nam sử lược (A Brief History of Vietnam), Sun Shiyi invested Lê Chiêu Thống as King of An Nam (An Nam quốc vương) right after entering the citadel.

## Enthronement at Phú Xuân
<!-- id: len-ngoi | kicker: 25th day of the 11th lunar month, 1788 -->

The news spread south. On the 25th day of the 11th lunar month of the year Mậu Thân, which is 22 December 1788, Nguyễn Huệ took the throne as emperor on Bân Mountain near Phú Xuân and adopted the reign name Quang Trung.

He then marched his army north, stopping in Nghệ An for ten days to recruit more troops. Việt Nam sử lược records that he had more than a hundred thousand troops and more than a hundred elephants; other sources give different estimates.

## Tam Điệp
<!-- id: tam-diep | kicker: End of the year Mậu Thân | interaction: tien-quan@0.9 | narration: Tam Điệp. At Tam Điệp, Quang Trung let his troops celebrate Tết early, then set out on the thirtieth day of the twelfth month. Drag the slider to send the army north. -->

The Tây Sơn army reached Tam Điệp. Quang Trung let his troops celebrate Tết early, then set out on the thirtieth day of the twelfth lunar month. He promised to enter Thăng Long and celebrate on the seventh day of the first month.

## The night at Hà Hồi
<!-- id: ha-hoi | kicker: Night of the 3rd day of the first month, Kỷ Dậu -->

On the night of the 3rd day of the first month of the year Kỷ Dậu, the Tây Sơn army suddenly closed in on the Hà Hồi post. According to the old chronicles, the Qing troops at Hà Hồi surrendered without a long fight.

The next night, the 4th, another column attacked the Khương Thượng post, where the Qing general Cen Yidong (Sầm Nghi Đống) was stationed.

## Ngọc Hồi
<!-- id: ngoc-hoi | kicker: 5th day of the first month, Kỷ Dậu -->

On the morning of the 5th, the Tây Sơn army attacked the Ngọc Hồi post. Việt Nam sử lược records that the Tây Sơn troops lashed boards together, covered them with wet straw, and advanced behind them as shields.

At the same time, the Khương Thượng post at Đống Đa was broken. Cen Yidong died there.

## Into Thăng Long
<!-- id: thang-long | kicker: 5th day of the first month, which is 30 January 1789 -->

During the 5th, the Tây Sơn army entered Thăng Long. Sun Shiyi fled north; the Qing army fell apart. Lê Chiêu Thống went with them into Qing territory.

After the victory, Quang Trung sent an envoy to make peace with the Qing court. Việt Nam sử lược records that the Qing court recognized him as King of An Nam and that the two sides kept up exchanges of envoys.
