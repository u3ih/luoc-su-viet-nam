# Bạch Đằng Once More (1288)

_Three centuries after Ngô Quyền, Trần Hưng Đạo reused the stratagem of hidden stakes and the tide on the Bạch Đằng River to defeat the Yuan fleet._

## Bạch Đằng Once More

Three centuries after Ngô Quyền, Trần Hưng Đạo reused the stratagem of hidden stakes and the tide on the Bạch Đằng River to defeat the Yuan fleet.

## The Yuan return
<!-- id: quan-nguyen | kicker: Late 1287 -->

In late 1287, the Yuan (Nguyên) army, commanded by Toghon (Thoát Hoan), invaded Đại Việt for the third time. The Yuan took Thăng Long, but the Trần court had withdrawn, and the Yuan could not capture the king and his court.

The main Yuan army sat in an abandoned capital. Its food supplies began to run short.

## Striking the supply fleet
<!-- id: van-don | kicker: Early 1288 -->

The deputy commander Trần Khánh Dư made a surprise attack on the supply fleet of Zhang Wenhu (Trương Văn Hổ) in the waters off Vân Đồn. The Yuan lost an important source of supplies.

Short of food, the Yuan could hardly hold Thăng Long for long.

## The Yuan withdraw
<!-- id: nguyen-rut | kicker: March 1288 -->

In early 1288, Toghon had to order a withdrawal from Thăng Long. One column went north by land. The naval column, commanded by Omar (Ô Mã Nhi), withdrew by river toward the sea, passing through the Bạch Đằng River.

Trần Hưng Đạo had foreseen this route of retreat.

## The hidden stakes
<!-- id: coc-ngam | kicker: Preparing the ground -->

Trần Hưng Đạo had stakes driven into the bed of the Bạch Đằng River. According to the old chronicles, the tips of the stakes were capped with sharp iron. He posted ambush troops on both banks to wait for the Yuan ships.

The plan relied on the tides of a great river mouth, and it had already been used at this place in 938.

## The tide
<!-- id: con-nuoc | kicker: The decisive battle | interaction: trieu-rut@0.85 | narration: The tide. According to the old chronicles, the Trần forces feigned defeat to lure the Yuan ships deep into the river at high tide. Drag the slider to let the tide fall. -->

According to the old chronicles, the Trần forces feigned defeat to lure the Yuan ships deep into the river while the tide was rising.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Interaction:** High tide ↔ Low tide · Drag right to let the tide fall
>
> As the water fell, the tips of the stakes rose above it. The Yuan ships were stuck, their formation fell into disorder, and the Trần forces struck from both banks. The battle lasted from morning to afternoon. The sources differ on the date of the battle (the 9th day of the 4th lunar month of the year Mậu Tý) and on details of how it unfolded.

## Victory
<!-- id: ket-qua | kicker: After the Bạch Đằng battle -->

Hundreds of Yuan warships were captured or destroyed. Omar and Phàn Tiếp were taken alive. Yuan losses ran to tens of thousands of men; the books give different figures.

The column that had withdrawn overland under Toghon was also attacked along the way and suffered heavy losses. The third resistance war came to an end.

## Traces beneath the river
<!-- id: dau-tich | kicker: Did you know? -->

Today, many fields of wooden stakes have been found in the river branches of the Quảng Ninh and Hải Phòng area. Researchers believe most of them belong to the Bạch Đằng battle of 1288, but where the main stake field lay is still debated, because the river has changed its course over the centuries.
